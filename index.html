About Founder

Nilesh Janardan Gaikwad

Founder & Publisher – GLOBAL IT PUBLICATIONS

Poet Author Computer Educationist Digital Marketing Consultant Web Developer Data Analytics Trainer M.A. Mass Communication & Journalism

Nilesh Janardan Gaikwad is a Poet, Author, Computer Educationist, Digital Marketing Consultant, Web Developer, Data Analytics Trainer and Publisher, with more than 20 years of experience in computer education and training.

With experience across education, technology, digital media and literature, he has established GLOBAL IT PUBLICATIONS with the objective of bringing together Book Publishing, Graphic Designing, Website Development, Digital Marketing, Data Analytics, Newspaper & Magazine Advertising and Educational Initiatives under one platform.

With an academic background in Mass Communication & Journalism and extensive practical experience in the digital and education sectors, he is also involved in conducting lectures and practical training in Multimedia & Mass Communication, Mass Communication & Journalism, Digital Marketing and related subjects.

As a Poet & Author

Nilesh Janardan Gaikwad is also actively associated with the field of Marathi literature. His poetry reflects a sensitive perspective towards life, experiences, emotions, nature, hope and human values.

His published poetry collection ‘Parv Usheche’ (पर्व उषेचे) represents an important milestone in his literary journey. Through this collection, he gives poetic expression to various experiences, emotions and observations of life.

As an Author

Drawing upon his experience in digital technology, education and digital marketing, he has authored the book:

‘Digital Marketing : Sankalpana, Sadhane ani Pratyakshik’

The book is based on a practical and learner-friendly approach.

Instead of presenting Digital Marketing only from a theoretical perspective, the book focuses on practical examples, demonstrations and step-by-step guidance to help learners understand and apply digital marketing concepts.

The book is particularly useful for students of Multimedia & Mass Communication, Mass Communication & Journalism, BCA and other academic disciplines that include Digital Marketing. It is also useful for students and young people aspiring to build a career in Digital Marketing, Freelancers, Entrepreneurs, Small Business Owners and Marketing Professionals.
